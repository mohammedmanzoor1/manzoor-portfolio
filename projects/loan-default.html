Dataset / source

255,347 loan records with borrower demographics, credit and financial attributes such as credit score and DTI, mortgage/co-signer status, plus loan amount, rate, term, and purpose.

Business problem / purpose

Why this analysis?

Identify key factors influencing borrower default and evaluate loan portfolio risk to support underwriting decisions, reduce credit risk, and improve portfolio performance.

What I did

From raw data to dashboard.

  1. Reviewed dataset structure and standardized column naming conventions.
  2. Created Credit Score Band, DTI Band, EMI-to-Income Ratio, and Tenure Stability derived fields.
  3. Ran SQL analysis to calculate default rates and evaluate risk across loan purpose, credit score, DTI, and employment stability.
  4. Built a Tableau dashboard with KPI cards, default-rate-by-purpose, credit-score risk, DTI risk, and employment-stability visuals, plus interactive filters.

Key insights

What the analysis showed.

  • Unstable-tenure borrowers default at 16.24% versus 10.46% for stable-tenure—the widest single-factor gap in the data.
  • Weaker credit profiles and higher DTI both correlate with higher default, though credit score alone is a fairly weak separator: 12.47% poor versus 10.18% excellent.
  • Combined risk factors compound sharply—Poor credit + Very High DTI + Unstable tenure reaches an 18.27% default rate, the highest segment identified.
