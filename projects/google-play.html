Dataset / source

Google Play Store application dataset with approximately 9K apps and install, rating, review, category, and pricing fields.

Business problem / purpose

Why this analysis?

Turn raw Google Play Store app data into business-facing views of app volume, install performance, category distribution, ratings, reviews, pricing type, and review sentiment to help identify high-performing categories and popular apps.

What I did

From raw data to dashboard.

  1. Reviewed and standardized category, app, pricing, install, rating, and review fields.
  2. Built a data model and DAX measures for app count, installs, ratings, reviews, free/paid split, and sentiment.
  3. Designed a four-page Power BI report: Executive Overview, App Performance, Pricing & Category, and Reviews & Sentiment.
  4. Added KPI cards, bar/donut charts, a bubble relationship view, and a category matrix table for interactive exploration.

Key insights

What the analysis showed.

  • Install activity is highly concentrated—Subway Surfers, Google News, Candy Crush Saga, and Temple Run 2 dominate.
  • Family has the most apps, but Game dominates review volume by a wide margin.
  • Free apps are approximately 92% of inventory, and the review-volume gap between free (2,273M) and paid (4M) apps is very large.
