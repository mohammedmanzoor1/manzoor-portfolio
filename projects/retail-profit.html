Dataset / source

Superstore_sql table with 11 fields including Order_ID, Order_Date, Customer_Name, Segment, Region, Category, Product_Name, Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit.

Business problem / purpose

Why this analysis?

Inspect, clean, and analyze retail order data, then build an interactive dashboard to track sales, profit, customer performance, monthly trends, category performance, and discount impact.

What I did

From raw data to dashboard.

  1. Retrieved and inspected the Superstore_sql table and its column/data types via INFORMATION_SCHEMA.
  2. Checked for null values in key fields and updated missing Profit to 0 and Customer_Name to Unknown.
  3. Checked for duplicate Order_IDs using GROUP BY / HAVING, then ran aggregate and window-based analysis.
  4. Built a Tableau dashboard with KPI cards, a customer treemap, a monthly trend line, category comparison, and a discount-impact chart, with Category, Segment, Region, and Quarter filters.

Key insights

What the analysis showed.

  • Deepak Mishra is the top customer at 17,256 in sales.
  • Monthly sales swing noticeably, from a low of 7,312 to a high of 19,438.
  • Higher discount groups show lower profit despite similar or higher sales, showing discounting erodes margin.
