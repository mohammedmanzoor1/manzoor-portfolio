Dataset / source

Online retail transactions from 2010–2011 with Invoice, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, Price, Customer_ID, and Country fields.

Business problem / purpose

Why this analysis?

Transform raw online retail transaction data into actionable insights on revenue performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and geographic sales distribution.

What I did

From raw data to dashboard.

  1. Cleaned data in Python by removing missing Customer IDs, converting InvoiceDate, and handling cancellations.
  2. Engineered Revenue (Quantity × Price), OrderType (Retail/Wholesale), and IsCancellation fields.
  3. Used SQL to answer 7 business questions covering revenue trend, top products, revenue by country, repeat-purchase rate, AOV trend, and revenue concentration.
  4. Built a Tableau dashboard with revenue trend, revenue by country, top products and customers, and UK-vs-international comparison, with KPI summary cards.

Key insights

What the analysis showed.

  • Revenue shows clear seasonal peaks, topping over 1.16M in November 2011.
  • The United Kingdom drives the large majority of revenue—14.7M of approximately 16.2M total—showing strong single-market dependence.
  • The top 20% of customers contribute 77.26% of total revenue, and repeat customers make up 72.39% of buyers.
