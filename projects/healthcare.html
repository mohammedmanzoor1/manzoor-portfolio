Dataset / source

100,000+ diabetic patient encounters across multiple US hospitals over 10 years, covering demographics, admission information, hospital utilization, diagnoses, medications, and readmission status.

Business problem / purpose

Why this analysis?

Analyze 30-day hospital readmission patterns among diabetic patients to identify risk factors and support better discharge planning and reduced avoidable readmissions.

What I did

From raw data to dashboard.

  1. Cleaned the data by replacing “?” with nulls, dropping high-missing columns and identifiers, handling duplicates, and standardizing types.
  2. Engineered a 30-Day Readmission Flag, Total Prior Visits, Long Stay Flag, Polypharmacy Flag, and High Utilizer Flag.
  3. Answered 8 business questions in SQL covering outcomes, age, length of stay, prior visits, admission type, discharge disposition, medications, and diagnosis count.
  4. Built a single-page Tableau dashboard with KPI cards and four core visualizations, filterable by race, gender, project and age.

Key insights

What the analysis showed.

  • Longer hospital stays and higher prior healthcare utilization are both associated with increased readmission risk.
  • Readmission rate rises fairly steadily with the number of diagnoses—from 5.94% at 1 diagnosis to 17.65% at 10.
  • Certain admission and discharge categories show much higher readmission rates than others; for example, disposition 3 is 14.66% versus disposition 1 at 9.3% on large patient counts.
